ABOUT

Discussion NGPT is the first of it’s kind in the world not generative pre-trained transformer, being not an artificial intelligence, and not generating anything new at all. First envisioned in 2026, the Discussion NGPT works to provide you your answers with as little work on the part of the NGPT as possible, and however much work you might be willing to put in.

The NGPT works by effectively processing your prompt, feeding it through a highly advanced algorithm, resulting in an entirely not-artificial result of searches, allowing you to gain usually relatively old information, the way your ancestors once did. Though avoiding the possibility of AI, results might be limited. You’re just going to have to suck it up though, because that’s not our problem.
